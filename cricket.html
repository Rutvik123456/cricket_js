CrickGame

📖 How to play
  • Pick Bat, Ball, or Stump by tapping a button.
  • The computer picks one at random at the same time.
  • Winner is decided like rock-paper-scissors:
    • Bat beats Ball
    • Ball beats Stump
    • Stump beats Bat
  • Same choice from both = a Tie.
  • Your Win / Lost / Tie totals are saved automatically, even if you refresh the page.
  • Click NEW GAME at any time to reset the scoreboard.
